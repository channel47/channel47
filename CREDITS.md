## Philosophy

We codify real practitioner knowledge into Claude plugins. The dtc-google-ads-playbook comes from managing a $6M+ Google Ads account. The dtc-research-engine comes from running customer voice research for dozens of DTC products. Every framework, threshold, and decision tree was battle-tested before it became a skill.

If you built something we were inspired by and we missed you — open an issue.
