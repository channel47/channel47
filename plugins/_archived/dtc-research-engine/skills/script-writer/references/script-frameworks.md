# Script Frameworks — Expanded Reference

## Hook Formulas (First 3-5 Seconds)

### Pattern Interrupt Hooks
These break the viewer's autopilot browsing by showing/saying something unexpected.

Formulas:
- "I was today years old when I found out [surprising fact about the problem]"
- "[Dramatic visual] + silence + then speak"
- "Stop scrolling if you [specific audience identifier]"
- "POV: you just discovered [product mechanism]"
- "The [unexpected profession] secret to [desired outcome]"

### Problem Call-Out Hooks
Directly name the viewer's pain. Works because people are drawn to content that "gets" them.

Formulas:
- "If your [problem symptom], this is for you"
- "That moment when [relatable frustrating situation]..."
- "Nobody talks about how [problem] actually affects your [deeper consequence]"
- "[Audience identifier]: we need to talk about [problem]"
- "The reason [common solution] doesn't work for [problem]"

### Curiosity Gap Hooks
Open a loop that can only be closed by watching. The most "scroll-stopping" category.

Formulas:
- "I found something that [vague incredible outcome] and I need to show you"
- "Wait until you see what happens when [setup]..."
- "There's a reason [unexpected claim] and nobody is talking about it"
- "[Number] out of [number] [audience] don't know this about [category]"
- "This changed everything for me and I almost didn't try it"

### Social Proof Hooks
Lead with evidence of others' success.

Formulas:
- "[Number] people can't be wrong about [product category]"
- "My [friend/sister/vet/doctor] told me about this and I didn't believe them"
- "I was skeptical until I saw [specific result]"
- "Every [audience identifier] I know is switching to this"

## UGC Script Templates

### The "Story Time" Template (60 sec)
```
[VISUAL: Talking head, casual, natural lighting]

HOOK (0-5 sec):
"Okay so I need to tell you about something because [relatable situation]..."

SETUP (5-15 sec):
"So basically, I've been dealing with [problem] for [timeframe].
I tried [solution 1], [solution 2], even [solution 3]."

BRIDGE (15-25 sec):
"Then my [friend/sister/etc] told me about this [product].
I was honestly skeptical because like I said, nothing has worked."

DEMO/PROOF (25-45 sec):
"But look at this — [show product / show result].
It literally [specific result] in [timeframe].
I'm not even exaggerating."

CTA (45-60 sec):
"If you're dealing with [problem], seriously just try this.
I'll put the link below. [Brand] has a [guarantee/offer] so
there's literally no risk."
```

### The "Get Ready With Me / Show & Tell" Template (45 sec)
```
[VISUAL: Using product in real environment, B-roll heavy]

HOOK (0-3 sec):
[TEXT OVERLAY: "The [product] that [bold claim]"]
"Okay watch this..."

DEMO (3-20 sec):
[Show product being used, real-time or time-lapse]
[Voice-over explaining what's happening]

REACTION (20-30 sec):
"See?? [Describe visible result]
I've been using this for [timeframe] and [sustained result]."

CTA (30-45 sec):
"The link is [in my bio / below]. Use code [CODE] for [offer]."
```

### The "Duet/Stitch Response" Template (30 sec)
```
[VISUAL: Stitching/responding to a common question in the category]

HOOK (0-3 sec):
"Someone asked [common question about the problem]..."

ANSWER (3-20 sec):
"Here's the thing — [brief answer].
But what actually works is [product mechanism].
[Quick proof/demo]"

CTA (20-30 sec):
"You're welcome. Link in bio."
```

## VSL Script Structure (5-10 min)

### Act 1: The Hook and Setup (0:00 - 1:30)

**Opening Hook** (0:00 - 0:30):
- Pattern interrupt or bold claim
- Must be so compelling they stay past the first 10 seconds
- Tease the "discovery" that's coming

**Problem Identification** (0:30 - 1:30):
- "If you're [audience identifier] who [specific situation]..."
- Describe their world — make them feel seen
- Use THEIR language from research data
- End with an open loop: "In the next few minutes, I'm going to show you..."

### Act 2: Agitation and Failed Solutions (1:30 - 3:30)

**Problem Expansion** (1:30 - 2:30):
- Cascade from surface pain to deeper consequences
- Use specific scenarios from research
- Build empathy, not guilt

**Failed Solutions** (2:30 - 3:30):
- Name 2-3 things they've tried
- Briefly explain WHY each failed (not just that they failed)
- This builds credibility: "this person understands my situation"

### Act 3: The Discovery (3:30 - 5:30)

**Bridge / Mechanism** (3:30 - 4:30):
- Introduce the breakthrough — HOW it works
- Lead with mechanism, not product name
- Make it sound genuinely novel

**Product Reveal** (4:30 - 5:30):
- Now name the product
- Connect mechanism to specific benefits
- Keep it benefits-focused, not feature-focused

### Act 4: Proof (5:30 - 7:30)

**Testimonial Stack** (5:30 - 6:30):
- 3-5 testimonial quotes or clips
- Vary the personas represented
- Include specific results and timeframes

**Authority / Science** (6:30 - 7:30):
- Expert endorsement if available
- Clinical/scientific backing if applicable
- Awards, media mentions, certifications

### Act 5: The Offer and Close (7:30 - 10:00)

**Offer Presentation** (7:30 - 8:30):
- What they get (main product)
- Bonuses (if any)
- Price anchoring: compare to alternatives or cost of problem
- The actual price

**Risk Reversal** (8:30 - 9:00):
- Money-back guarantee
- Free trial if applicable
- Remove every possible reason to say "not now"

**Final CTA** (9:00 - 10:00):
- Summarize the transformation they'll experience
- Paint the "after" picture vividly
- Tell them exactly what to do
- Create gentle urgency

## YouTube Pre-Roll (15 sec Non-Skippable)

```
[SECOND 1-3]: HOOK
[Bold text overlay + voice: "[Specific problem]?"]

[SECOND 4-8]: SOLUTION
[Show product / mechanism briefly]
[Voice: "[Product] [does X] in [timeframe]"]

[SECOND 9-12]: PROOF
[Quick social proof: "[Number] five-star reviews"]

[SECOND 13-15]: CTA
[Voice: "Try it risk-free at [URL]"]
[VISUAL: Product + URL + offer]
```

## Platform-Specific Timing Guide

| Platform | Format | Ideal Length | Hook Window |
|----------|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Meta Feed | UGC Video | 30-60 sec | 3 seconds |
| Meta Stories | Vertical Video | 15 sec | 1-2 seconds |
| TikTok | Native Video | 15-60 sec | 1-2 seconds |
| YouTube Pre-Roll | Skippable | 15-30 sec | 5 seconds (skip button) |
| YouTube In-Stream | Long-form | 2-5 min | 5-10 seconds |
| Landing Page VSL | Sales Video | 5-15 min | 10-15 seconds |

## Word Count to Time Conversion

Average speaking pace for ads:
- Casual/conversational: ~130-150 words per minute
- Energetic/fast-paced: ~160-180 words per minute
- Slow/dramatic: ~100-120 words per minute

| Target Duration | Casual WPM | Energetic WPM |
|----------------|------------|---------------|
| 15 seconds | ~35 words | ~45 words |
| 30 seconds | ~70 words | ~90 words |
| 60 seconds | ~140 words | ~170 words |
| 90 seconds | ~210 words | ~260 words |
| 3 minutes | ~420 words | ~510 words |
| 5 minutes | ~700 words | ~850 words |
