# DTC Copy Formulas & Frameworks

## Primary Text Formulas (Meta Ads)

### Formula 1: Problem-Agitate-Solve (PAS)
```
[Problem — state the pain in their words]
[Agitate — deepen the pain with consequences]
[Solve — introduce the product as the answer]
[Proof — one testimonial or data point]
[CTA — what to do next]
```

Example:
"Hard water stains that won't come off no matter how much you scrub?
You've probably wasted hours with Clorox and a pumice stone — and the ring comes back in a week.
[Product] dissolves hard water stains on contact. Just drop it in and walk away.
Over 50,000 five-star reviews can't be wrong.
Tap 'Shop Now' to try it risk-free."

### Formula 2: Before-After-Bridge (BAB)
```
[Before — describe their current painful reality]
[After — paint the picture of life with the problem solved]
[Bridge — the product is how they get from before to after]
```

### Formula 3: The Testimonial Lead
```
"[Strong customer testimonial quote]"

[Expand on what the customer experienced]
[Introduce the product]
[Offer + CTA]
```

### Formula 4: The "If-Then" Hook
```
If you [specific situation the target is in]...
Then you need to know about [product/mechanism].

[Explain why]
[Proof]
[CTA]
```

### Formula 5: The List Format
```
[Hook question or statement]

Here's what makes [product] different:
- [Benefit 1 — in customer language]
- [Benefit 2]
- [Benefit 3]

[Social proof]
[CTA]
```

### Formula 6: The Story Ad
```
"[Personal story opening — relatable situation]"

[Brief problem description]
[How they discovered the product]
[Result they experienced]

[Product name + offer]
[CTA]
```

## Headline Formulas

### Benefit-Driven
- "[Desired outcome] in [timeframe]"
- "The [adjective] way to [desired outcome]"
- "Finally, [desired outcome] without [pain point]"
- "[Specific result] guaranteed"

### Curiosity-Driven
- "The [category] secret [audience] are discovering"
- "Why [specific audience] are switching to [product]"
- "What [number] customers know that you don't"
- "The [unexpected fact] about [category]"

### Social Proof-Driven
- "[Number] [audience] can't be wrong"
- "The #1 rated [category] on [platform]"
- "[Authority figure] recommended"
- "As seen in [media outlet]"

### Problem-Driven
- "Tired of [specific pain point]?"
- "Still dealing with [problem]?"
- "[Problem]? There's a better way"
- "The [problem] solution you haven't tried yet"

### Offer-Driven
- "[Discount]% off — today only"
- "Try [product] risk-free"
- "Free [bonus] with every order"
- "[Price] for [product] (worth [higher price])"

## Google Search Ad Copy Patterns

### Headline Patterns (30 char max)
Type 1 — Brand: "BarkShield Dog Trainer"
Type 2 — Benefit: "Stop Barking in Minutes"
Type 3 — CTA: "Try Risk-Free Today"
Type 4 — Proof: "50,000+ Happy Customers"
Type 5 — Offer: "60% Off - Limited Time"
Type 6 — Feature: "Safe & Humane Training"
Type 7 — Question: "Dog Won't Stop Barking?"
Type 8 — Urgency: "Sale Ends Tonight"

### Description Patterns (90 char max)
Type 1 — Problem-Solution: "Struggling with [problem]? [Product] [benefit] in [timeframe]. Try it risk-free."
Type 2 — Social Proof: "Join [number] happy customers. [Product] is rated [rating] stars. Free shipping."
Type 3 — Offer Stack: "Get [product] + [bonus] for just [price]. [Guarantee]. Order now."
Type 4 — Differentiation: "Unlike [alternative], [product] [unique mechanism]. See results in [timeframe]."

## Email Subject Line Formulas

### Curiosity
- "I wasn't going to share this, but..."
- "The [category] mistake 90% of people make"
- "This changed everything for my [situation]"
- "[Surprising claim] (here's the proof)"

### Benefit
- "How to [desired outcome] in [timeframe]"
- "[Number] ways to [desired outcome] starting today"
- "Your [problem] ends here"

### Pain
- "Still dealing with [problem]?"
- "The [problem] nobody talks about"
- "Why [common solution] is making your [problem] worse"

### Social Proof
- "[Number] people did this last week"
- "The [product] that [audience] can't stop talking about"
- "See why [product] has [number] five-star reviews"

### Urgency
- "Last chance: [offer] ends at midnight"
- "[Number] left in stock"
- "Your [discount] expires in [timeframe]"

### Story
- "A customer just sent us this..."
- "I almost didn't believe this review"
- "What happened when [customer name] tried [product]"

## Power Words for DTC Copy

### Urgency Words
limited, exclusive, only, now, today, last chance, hurry, while supplies last, don't miss, act fast

### Trust Words
guaranteed, proven, certified, trusted, verified, tested, backed, endorsed, recommended

### Benefit Words
instant, effortless, automatic, simple, easy, fast, powerful, effective, permanent, complete

### Emotion Words
finally, imagine, discover, secret, breakthrough, revolutionary, transform, unlock, escape, freedom

### Specificity Words
exactly, precisely, specifically, [exact number], in [exact time], every single, down to the last

## Anti-Patterns (What to Avoid)

### Cliches to Ban
- "Are you tired of..."
- "Introducing the revolutionary..."
- "Say goodbye to..."
- "What if I told you..."
- "But wait, there's more!"

These are so overused they trigger ad blindness. If you must use one, subvert it.

### Weak Copy Signals
- **Vague benefits**: "improve your life" — compared to what?
- **Feature-dumping**: listing specs instead of benefits
- **Missing proof**: claims without evidence
- **Generic audience**: writing to "everyone" instead of one specific person
- **Passive voice**: "results were seen" instead of "you'll see results"
- **Corporate tone**: "our solution leverages" — write like a human
