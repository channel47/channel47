# Platform Specifications — Ad Copy Character Limits & Best Practices

## Meta (Facebook / Instagram)

### Feed Ads
- **Primary Text**: 125 characters visible before "See More" (up to 2,200 total)
- **Headline**: 40 characters recommended (max 255)
- **Description**: 30 characters recommended (max 255)
- **URL Display**: Auto-populated from link

Best practices:
- Front-load the hook in the first 125 characters — most people won't click "See More"
- Use line breaks to create scannable structure in long-form primary text
- Headlines should work independently of the primary text
- Test short (1-2 line) vs. long (paragraph+) primary text

### Stories / Reels Ads
- **Primary Text**: 125 characters (less visible — the creative carries the message)
- **Overlay Text**: Keep to 20% of image/video area
- Text should be in the "safe zone" — center 1080x1420 area of 1080x1920

### Carousel Ads
- **Primary Text**: Same as feed (125 visible)
- **Card Headline**: 40 characters each
- **Card Description**: 20 characters each
- Up to 10 cards

## Google Ads

### Responsive Search Ads (RSA)
- **Headlines**: Up to 15 (min 3), 30 characters each
- **Descriptions**: Up to 4 (min 2), 90 characters each
- **Display Path**: 2 fields, 15 characters each
- Headlines can be pinned to positions 1, 2, or 3
- Google mixes and matches — write each headline to work independently

RSA best practices:
- Include brand name in at least 2 headlines
- Include primary keyword in at least 3 headlines
- Mix CTAs, benefits, features, and social proof across headlines
- Each description should be a complete, standalone selling point
- Don't repeat the same message in multiple headlines

### Responsive Display Ads
- **Short Headlines**: Up to 5, 30 characters each
- **Long Headline**: 1, 90 characters
- **Descriptions**: Up to 5, 90 characters each
- **Business Name**: 25 characters
- **CTA**: Selected from predefined options (Shop Now, Learn More, etc.)

### YouTube Ads
- **Headline**: 15 characters (display)
- **Long Headline**: 90 characters
- **Description**: 2 descriptions, 90 characters each
- **CTA**: Selected from options
- **Companion Banner**: 300x60 pixels

### Performance Max
- **Headlines**: Up to 5, 30 characters each
- **Long Headlines**: Up to 5, 90 characters each
- **Descriptions**: Up to 5 (1 must be 60 chars or less), 90 characters each

## TikTok

### In-Feed Ads
- **Ad Caption**: 1-100 characters (plus hashtags)
- **Display Name**: 2-20 characters
- **CTA Button**: Selected from options (Shop Now, Learn More, Sign Up, etc.)

Best practices:
- Shorter captions perform better — let the video do the work
- Include 2-3 relevant hashtags
- Native language > polished ad copy
- Emojis are acceptable and often perform well
- Text overlays on video: 5-8 words max per screen

### Spark Ads (boosted organic)
- Uses the original post caption — write it like an organic post, not an ad
- Hashtags matter more here for discoverability

## Email

### Subject Lines
- **Optimal length**: 30-50 characters (displays fully on most devices)
- **Max before truncation**: ~60 characters on desktop, ~30 on mobile
- **Preview text**: 40-90 characters (appears after subject on most clients)

Best practices:
- Front-load key info before character 30
- Personalization tokens ([First Name]) add ~10 characters
- Emojis: use sparingly, test performance (one max)
- Avoid all caps and excessive punctuation (spam filters)
- Preview text should extend the subject line, not repeat it

## Landing Page Copy

### Hero Section
- **Headline**: 8-12 words (under 60 characters ideal)
- **Subheadline**: 15-25 words
- **CTA Button**: 2-5 words

### Section Headers
- 5-10 words each
- Should tell a complete story when read in sequence (without body text)

### Body Copy
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- One idea per paragraph
- Use bold for scannable key phrases

### CTA Buttons
- Use action verbs: "Get", "Try", "Start", "Claim"
- Include the value: "Get My Free Trial" > "Submit"
- Urgency words: "Now", "Today", "Limited"
- Good examples: "Try It Risk-Free", "Get 50% Off Today", "Start My Free Trial"
