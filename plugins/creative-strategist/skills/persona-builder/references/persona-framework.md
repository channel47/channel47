# Persona Framework — Calibration Examples

These examples show the depth and specificity expected from data-driven personas.

---

## Example 1: Pet Training Device

### Persona: "The Desperate Dog Mom"

**The Snapshot**
- Age Range: 30-45
- Gender Skew: ~70% female
- Life Situation: Works from home, dog is a rescue or COVID adoption. Apartment/townhouse with shared walls.
- Income Signal: Willing to spend $30-80 but wants proof first. Already spent money on training that didn't work.

**The Internal Monologue**
> "I love my dog so much but I'm at my breaking point. He barks at EVERYTHING. I've watched every YouTube video, tried the trainer treats, even hired a trainer for $200 and he was good for like a week. My neighbor left a note on my door last week and I was honestly mortified. I just need something that actually works."

**The Trigger Event**
- Primary: Neighbor complaint (note, awkward encounter, formal complaint)
- Secondary: Embarrassment during walks, unable to have guests over

**Pain Points (ranked)**
1. Social embarrassment — "my neighbors must hate me"
2. Feeling like a failure as a pet owner
3. Disruption to daily life — can't work from home in peace

**Desired Outcome**
- Stated: "I want my dog to stop barking"
- Deeper: "I want to enjoy having a dog again and not feel constant anxiety"

**Objections**
1. "These things never work — I've tried everything"
2. "Is it going to hurt my dog?"
3. "It probably only works for some dogs, not mine"

**Language Fingerprint**
- "I've tried everything"
- "at my breaking point"
- "my neighbor complained"
- "does this actually work?"
- "game changer"

**Ad Responsiveness**
- Hook: Social proof + empathy
- Proof: Video demonstrations, real testimonials
- CTA: Risk-reversal ("30 days, full refund if it doesn't work")

## Example 2: Toilet Cleaning Product

### Persona: "The Grossed-Out Husband"

**The Snapshot**
- Age Range: 28-40
- Gender Skew: ~55% male (often assumed female — data says otherwise)
- Life Situation: Lives with partner. Recently became responsible for bathroom cleaning.
- Income Signal: Not price-sensitive for cleaning products. Cares about effectiveness.

**The Internal Monologue**
> "I didn't realize how disgusting toilet rings were until I actually started cleaning the bathroom. The regular cleaner doesn't do anything. I've been scrubbing for 20 minutes and it still looks nasty. There has to be something that just handles this without me getting in there with a brush every week."

**The Trigger Event**
- Primary: Visible stain that won't come off
- Secondary: Guests coming over, partner's comment

**Pain Points (ranked)**
1. Regular products don't clean tough stains
2. Scrubbing is gross and time-consuming
3. Embarrassment when guests use the bathroom

**Desired Outcome**
- Stated: "I want a clean toilet without effort"
- Deeper: "I want to feel like I have my life together without spending it cleaning"

**Language Fingerprint**
- "nothing works on hard water stains"
- "I don't want to scrub"
- "is this legit or another gimmick?"
- "drop it in and forget about it"

**Ad Responsiveness**
- Hook: Demonstration-led
- Proof: Video showing product working in real-time
- CTA: Low-friction curiosity

## Calibration Notes

When building personas, check against these examples for:
1. **Specificity** — specific life details, not generic demographics
2. **Internal monologue** — casual first-person using real language patterns
3. **Pain hierarchy** — ranked, not just listed
4. **Stated vs. deeper desire** — always separate these
5. **Specific objections** — not "price concern" but the actual objection in their words
6. **Language fingerprint** — exact phrases, not paraphrased
7. **Ad responsiveness** — directly actionable for creative
