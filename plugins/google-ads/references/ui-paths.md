# Google Ads UI Paths

Step-by-step navigation paths for actions recommended by google-ads plugin skills. Copy these into action plans.

## Keywords

### Pause a keyword
Google Ads > Campaigns > [Campaign Name] > Ad groups > [Ad Group Name] > Keywords > check keyword > Edit > Pause

### Change keyword bid
Google Ads > Campaigns > [Campaign Name] > Ad groups > [Ad Group Name] > Keywords > click bid column > enter new bid > Save

## Negative Keywords

### Add campaign-level negatives
Google Ads > Campaigns > [Campaign Name] > Keywords > Negative keywords > + > Add negative keywords > select "Campaign" level > paste keywords > Save

### Add ad-group-level negatives
Google Ads > Campaigns > [Campaign Name] > Ad groups > [Ad Group Name] > Keywords > Negative keywords > + > paste keywords > Save

### Create or add to shared negative keyword list
Google Ads > Tools & Settings > Shared library > Negative keyword lists > + or select existing list > Add keywords > paste keywords > Save

### Apply shared list to campaigns
Google Ads > Tools & Settings > Shared library > Negative keyword lists > select list > Apply to campaigns > check campaigns > Save

## Campaigns

### Pause a campaign
Google Ads > Campaigns > check campaign > Edit > Pause

### Adjust campaign budget
Google Ads > Campaigns > [Campaign Name] > Settings > Budget > enter new daily budget > Save

### Disable Display network on Search campaign
Google Ads > Campaigns > [Campaign Name] > Settings > Networks > uncheck "Google Display Network" > Save

## Performance Max

### Add PMax brand exclusions
Google Ads > Campaigns > [PMax Campaign Name] > Settings > Brand exclusions > + Add brand exclusions > search and select brands > Save

### Add PMax campaign-level negative keywords
Google Ads > Tools & Settings > Account-level negative keywords (or contact Google rep for campaign-level PMax negatives)

Note: PMax campaign-level negative keywords require applying via Google Ads API or requesting from Google support. Account-level negatives are accessible in the UI.

## Ad Copy

### Edit RSA headlines/descriptions
Google Ads > Campaigns > [Campaign Name] > Ad groups > [Ad Group Name] > Ads > click ad > Edit > modify headlines/descriptions > Save

### Check ad strength
Google Ads > Campaigns > [Campaign Name] > Ad groups > [Ad Group Name] > Ads > view "Ad strength" column

### Pin/unpin RSA assets
Google Ads > Campaigns > [Campaign Name] > Ad groups > [Ad Group Name] > Ads > click ad > Edit > click pin icon next to headline/description > select position or remove pin > Save

## Extensions / Assets

### Add sitelinks
Google Ads > Ads & assets > Assets > + > Sitelink > fill in details > Save

### Add callouts
Google Ads > Ads & assets > Assets > + > Callout > fill in details > Save

## Reporting

### View auction insights
Google Ads > Campaigns (or Ad groups or Keywords) > select entities > Auction insights

### View search terms report
Google Ads > Campaigns > [Campaign Name] > Keywords > Search terms

### View change history
Google Ads > Change history (left sidebar)
