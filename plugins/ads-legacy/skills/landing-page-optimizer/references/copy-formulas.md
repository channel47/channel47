# Copy Formulas & Templates

Reusable patterns for headlines, CTAs, testimonials, and key sections. Based on proven direct response frameworks.

---

## Headline Formulas

### Pain-First Headlines

Lead with the problem, not the solution. These work best for problem-aware and solution-aware audiences.

| Formula | Example |
|---------|---------|
| **[Pain] → [Solution]** | "Tired of bad breath? Fix it at the source." |
| **"Ever notice [pain indicator]?"** | "Ever notice people step back when you talk?" |
| **"If you [pain], you're not alone"** | "If you carry mints everywhere, you're not alone" |
| **"Stop [frustrating action]"** | "Stop hiding your smile in photos" |
| **"Why [common solution] doesn't work"** | "Why mouthwash doesn't fix bad breath" |

### Curiosity Headlines

Open loops that demand closure. Use sparingly — overuse feels clickbait-y.

| Formula | Example |
|---------|---------|
| **"The [unexpected source] of [problem]"** | "The real source of bad breath isn't your mouth" |
| **"What [experts] don't tell you about [topic]"** | "What dentists don't tell you about breath mints" |
| **"[Number] [people] discovered [unexpected truth]"** | "12,847 people discovered their gut was the problem" |
| **"The [timeframe] fix for [problem]"** | "The 14-day fix for chronic bad breath" |

### Proof-Led Headlines

Lead with credibility. Best for product-aware audiences who need reassurance.

| Formula | Example |
|---------|---------|
| **"[Specific number] [people] [outcome]"** | "847 customers went from avoiding conversations to confidence" |
| **"Why [authority] recommends [product]"** | "Why doctors recommend gut probiotics for breath" |
| **"[Case study result] in [timeframe]"** | "Fresh breath in 14 days — verified by 12,000+ customers" |
| **"Join [number]+ [people] who [outcome]"** | "Join 100,000+ people who fixed their breath from the inside" |

### Comparison Headlines

Position against alternatives. Works for competitor-aware audiences.

| Formula | Example |
|---------|---------|
| **"[Product] vs [Alternative]: [key difference]"** | "Probiotics vs Mouthwash: One masks, one fixes" |
| **"The [alternative] that actually works"** | "The breath solution that actually works" |
| **"Why [your approach] beats [common approach]"** | "Why gut health beats tongue scrapers" |
| **"Forget [old solution]"** | "Forget mints. Fix the source." |

## Subheadline Formulas

Subheadlines should overcome the primary objection to the headline or add crucial context.

| Headline Type | Subheadline Should... | Example |
|---------------|----------------------|---------|
| **Pain-first** | Promise the solution | "A 14-day protocol that targets the bacteria in your gut, not your mouth" |
| **Curiosity** | Satisfy partially, promise more | "The problem isn't your oral hygiene. It's what's happening in your digestive system." |
| **Proof-led** | Add credibility layer | "Verified purchases. Real results. 60-day money-back guarantee." |
| **Comparison** | State the key difference | "Mouthwash masks for 20 minutes. This fixes it for good." |

## Testimonial Templates

### The Timeline Testimonial

Best for products with progressive results.

```
"[Skepticism or starting point].

Day [X]: [First observation]
Day [Y]: [Notable change]
Day [Z]: [Breakthrough moment]

[Timeframe] later, [transformation + emotional impact]."

— [Full Name], [Location], Verified Purchase
```

**Example:**
```
"I was skeptical after trying 4 other probiotics.

Day 3: Nothing.
Day 7: Morning breath wasn't as bad.
Day 14: My wife said I could finally kiss her in the morning again.

Three months later, I stopped carrying mints everywhere."

— Marcus T., Portland OR, Verified Purchase
```

### The Before/After Testimonial

Best for transformational products.

```
"Before [Product], I [pain/struggle].

[Specific moment of realization or turning point].

Now I [new reality]. [Specific behavior change or outcome]."

— [Full Name], [Location], Verified Purchase
```

**Example:**
```
"Before Best Breath, I avoided close conversations. I'd step back,
cover my mouth when laughing, turn away when talking to coworkers.

My nurse friend told me about this. Her doctor husband takes it.
I figured I'd try one more thing.

Now I don't think about my breath anymore. I just... talk to people.
Like a normal person."

— Jennifer K., Austin TX, Verified Purchase
```

### The Skeptic-Converted Testimonial

Best for products with incredulous claims.

```
"I didn't believe [claim]. [Why I was skeptical].

But [what made me try it anyway].

[Specific result that surprised me]. [Emotional impact]."

— [Full Name], [Location], Verified Purchase
```

**Example:**
```
"I didn't believe a probiotic could fix breath. I've tried everything —
the problem was clearly in my mouth, right?

But my coworker wouldn't shut up about it. So I tried it to prove her wrong.

Week two, my girlfriend noticed before I did. Said my breath just...
didn't smell like anything. That's never happened."

— David R., Chicago IL, Verified Purchase
```

## Section Templates

### "How It Works" Section

```
## Why This Works When [Common Approach] Doesn't

You've tried [common solutions]. They don't work because [reason].

[Simple explanation of the real cause — 2-3 sentences max].

[Product] works by [mechanism in plain language].

**Timeline:**
- Week 1: [First thing they'll notice]
- Week 2-3: [Progressive improvement]
- Week 4+: [Full transformation]
```

### "Why Now" Section (Urgency)

```
## Why People Are Stocking Up

[Specific number] people bought [Product] during [time period].

[Reason for urgency — limited stock, price increase, seasonal demand].

[Social proof of others acting now].

[CTA with urgency element]
```

### "Risk Reversal" Section

```
## Try It Risk-Free

[Guarantee type] — [Timeframe].

If [Product] doesn't [specific outcome], [what happens].

No [common friction point]. No [another friction point].

[Number] customers have used this guarantee. [Low percentage] actually do.

[CTA]
```

## Reading Level Guidelines

Copy at 5th-7th grade reading level converts at **11.1%** vs 5.3% for college level.

### Quick Fixes

| College Level | 5th Grade Level |
|---------------|-----------------|
| "Utilize" | "Use" |
| "Facilitate" | "Help" |
| "Implement" | "Do" or "Start" |
| "Subsequently" | "Then" |
| "Approximately" | "About" |
| "Regarding" | "About" |
| "In order to" | "To" |
| "At this point in time" | "Now" |
| "Due to the fact that" | "Because" |

### Sentence Length

- **Average:** 14-17 words per sentence
- **Mix:** Short sentences (5-8 words) with medium (15-20)
- **Max:** Rarely exceed 25 words

### The Hemingway Test

Run final copy through hemingwayapp.com. Target:
- Grade 5-7 reading level
- Few adverbs
- Few passive voice uses
- Short sentences highlighted
