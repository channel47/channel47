# Before/After Copy Transformations

Real examples of landing page copy optimization demonstrating the core principles.

## Example 1: Hero Section (Listicle/Advertorial)

### Before (Generic, Corporate)

```
Discover the Top 5 Clinically-Backed Probiotic Solutions for Bad Breath in 2024

Updated: January 2026 — Based on comprehensive testing and analysis

Bad breath doesn't just affect your confidence—it impacts your relationships,
professional success, and overall quality of life. With countless products
claiming to eliminate oral bacteria and freshen breath, finding a solution
that actually works can feel overwhelming.
```

### Problems Identified

| Issue | Why It Fails |
|-------|--------------|
| Em dash | "doesn't just X—it impacts Y" is AI signature |
| Generic benefits | "confidence," "relationships," "professional success" — could be any product |
| Corporate tone | "comprehensive testing and analysis" — sounds like a press release |
| No customer language | Nothing a real person would say |
| Too long | 62 words before getting to value |

### After (Customer Language, Pain-First)

```
Best Bad Breath Probiotics of 2025

Updated: January 2026

Ever notice people touching their nose when they sit next to you?
Or taking a sudden deep breath and sighing?

I spent 10 years trying everything. Mouthwash, tongue scrapers, mint
after mint. The problem wasn't my mouth. It was my gut.

I tested 5 probiotics that claim to fix bad breath at the source.
One worked so well I stopped carrying mints everywhere.

Below are my Top 5 Picks, with a full review of the winner at the bottom.
```

### What Changed

| Principle Applied | How |
|-------------------|-----|
| **Customer language** | "touching their nose," "taking a deep breath" — from Reddit threads |
| **Pain-first opening** | Present-tense, relatable scenario before solution |
| **Specificity** | "10 years," "5 probiotics" — not "many years" or "several products" |
| **Editorial voice** | "I tested..." — matches listicle/advertorial format |
| **Concrete outcome** | "stopped carrying mints everywhere" — specific transformation |
| **No AI tells** | No em dashes, no corporate transitions |

## Example 2: Product Benefits Section

### Before (Feature Dump, Jargon)

```
Advanced Digestive Support

Unlike topical solutions that mask symptoms, Best Breath addresses the root
cause: digestive imbalance. By improving gut permeability and promoting
healthy bacterial flora, this formula works from the inside out to eliminate
odor-causing compounds before they reach your mouth.
```

### Problems Identified

- Colon after "root cause" (AI tell)
- Jargon: "gut permeability," "bacterial flora"
- No customer language
- Missing specific benefit
- Corporate/clinical tone

### After (Benefit-Focused, Conversational)

```
Fixes the Real Problem (Not Your Mouth)

Most people with chronic bad breath have tried everything for their mouth.
Scraped their tongue raw. Used mouthwash that burns. Chewed so much gum
their jaw hurt.

None of it works because the smell isn't coming from your mouth.
It's coming from your gut.

Best Breath has 67.5 Million CFU of 6 probiotic strains that target the
bacteria producing sulfur compounds in your digestive system. The ones
creating that smell that follows you like a cloud.
```

### What Changed

| Principle Applied | How |
|-------------------|-----|
| **Pain acknowledgment** | Lists what they've already tried (shows understanding) |
| **Customer language** | "scraped their tongue raw," "smell that follows you like a cloud" |
| **Specificity** | 67.5M CFU, 6 strains — not "proprietary blend" |
| **Simple explanation** | "sulfur compounds" instead of "bacterial flora" |
| **Conversational rhythm** | Short sentences mixed with longer ones (burstiness) |

## Example 3: Testimonial (FAQ Format → CRAVENS)

### Before (Generic FAQ)

```
Q: How quickly does Best Breath work?

A: Most users report noticeable improvements within 3-7 days of consistent use.
For best results, take one tablet daily as directed.
```

### Problems Identified

- Generic ("most users")
- No verification or credibility
- Corporate FAQ format
- No emotional connection
- No specificity

### After (CRAVENS Format)

```
"I've been fighting this for the better part of a decade. People who pass
by me would cough. People sitting next to me would touch their nose or
take deep breaths. I tried every probiotic on the market.

Day 3 on Best Breath: Nothing.
Day 7: Morning breath wasn't as bad.
Day 14: My wife said she didn't notice anything when I talked to her
in the morning. First time she's said that in years.

Three months later, I don't carry mints anymore. I don't think about
my breath before every conversation."

— Marcus T., Portland OR, Verified Purchase
```

### CRAVENS Analysis

| Element | How Applied |
|---------|-------------|
| **C**redible | Full name, location, verified purchase |
| **R**elevant | Describes same struggle target audience has |
| **A**ttractive (emotional) | "fighting this for a decade," wife comment |
| **V**isual | Could add photo |
| **E**numerated | Day 3, Day 7, Day 14 timeline |
| **N**earby | "Portland OR" — real location |
| **S**pecific | "First time in years," "three months later" |

## Example 4: CTA Section

### Before (Generic, Corporate)

```
[Visit Official Site]

Don't wait—the current 50% discount won't last forever. Secure your supply
today and experience the confidence that comes with fresh breath and optimal
digestive health.
```

### Problems Identified

- Generic CTA ("Visit Official Site")
- Em dash ("Don't wait—")
- Corporate language ("Secure your supply," "optimal digestive health")
- No first-person ownership
- Vague urgency

### After (First-Person, Specific)

```
[Get My 50% Discount]

12,847 people bought during this sale. Stock runs out regularly.

If you're tired of thinking about your breath before every conversation,
this is the fix. Not a mask. The actual fix.
```

### What Changed

| Principle Applied | How |
|-------------------|-----|
| **First-person CTA** | "Get My" creates ownership |
| **Specific social proof** | 12,847 — not "thousands" |
| **Customer language** | "thinking about your breath before every conversation" |
| **Differentiation** | "Not a mask. The actual fix." — clear positioning |
| **Shorter** | 38 words vs 42 — punchier |

## Example 5: How It Works Section

### Before (Corporate Explanation)

```
How Best Breath Works

Our proprietary probiotic formula is designed to optimize your digestive
microbiome. By introducing beneficial bacteria into your gastrointestinal
tract, Best Breath helps maintain a healthy balance of gut flora, which
in turn supports fresher breath from within.

Key Benefits:
• Supports digestive health
• Promotes oral freshness
• All-natural ingredients
• Made in USA
```

### Problems Identified

- "Our proprietary" — me-focused
- Jargon: "microbiome," "gastrointestinal tract," "gut flora"
- Generic benefits: "supports," "promotes"
- No specificity
- No transformation language

### After (Customer-Focused, Specific)

```
Why This Works When Nothing Else Did

You've tried cleaning your mouth. It doesn't work because the smell
isn't in your mouth.

Bad breath bacteria live in your gut. They produce sulfur compounds
that come up through your digestive system and out through your
breath — even your pores.

Best Breath puts 67.5 Million CFU of 6 probiotic strains directly
into your digestive system. These good bacteria crowd out the ones
causing the smell.

Week 1: Morning breath improves
Week 2-3: Daytime breath normalizes
Week 4+: The problem stops crossing your mind
```

### What Changed

| Principle Applied | How |
|-------------------|-----|
| **Pain acknowledgment** | "You've tried cleaning your mouth. It doesn't work." |
| **Simple science** | "sulfur compounds" not "gastrointestinal tract" |
| **Specificity** | 67.5M CFU, 6 strains, weekly timeline |
| **Outcome focus** | "The problem stops crossing your mind" — transformation |
| **Timeline expectations** | Week 1, Week 2-3, Week 4+ — sets realistic expectations |

## Example 6: Price Justification Section

### Before (Defensive, Generic)

```
What is the price?

Medical-grade probiotics regularly sell for $50+ in fancy health food
and supplement stores. Best Breath is normally priced at $40. But right
now they're offering a 50% discount!

Since the company is not well known (yet!) and doesn't invest much
money in marketing, it's offering a such an affordable price.
```

### Problems Identified

- Defensive tone about price
- "fancy health food" — awkward
- Parenthetical "(yet!)" — unprofessional
- Grammar error ("a such an affordable")
- No anchoring to value received

### After (Value-Anchored)

```
$20 for Fresh Breath Confidence

What's the cost of:
- Mints and gum every day? ~$15/month
- Mouthwash that doesn't work? ~$8/month
- Avoiding conversations? Priceless anxiety

Best Breath is $20 for a month's supply (normally $40 — 50% off right now).

One month to test if this actually works. If it doesn't, you're out $20.
If it does, you stop the mint-before-every-conversation routine for good.
```

### What Changed

| Principle Applied | How |
|-------------------|-----|
| **Value anchoring** | Compares to ongoing costs they already pay |
| **Pain language** | "Avoiding conversations? Priceless anxiety" |
| **Risk reversal framing** | "If it doesn't, you're out $20" — minimizes risk |
| **Transformation** | "stop the mint-before-every-conversation routine" |
| **Specificity** | $15/month, $8/month — concrete comparisons |

## Pattern Summary

### Transformation Patterns by Problem Type

| Original Problem | Solution Pattern |
|------------------|------------------|
| Corporate/formal tone | Add contractions, questions, short sentences |
| Feature dumping | So What? ladder → emotional outcome |
| Generic claims | Add specific numbers (847, 14 days, 67.5M) |
| Vague testimonials | CRAVENS format with timeline |
| Me-focused copy | Flip to you-focused ("You've tried...") |
| Em dashes | Replace with periods, vary rhythm |
| AI buzzwords | Simple words (use, get, fix vs leverage, unlock, optimize) |
| Generic CTAs | First-person + outcome ("Get My Fresh Breath Fix") |

### Reading Level Check

Run copy through Hemingway Editor or similar:
- Target: Grade 5-7 reading level
- Result: 11.1% conversion vs 5.3% for college level (2x improvement)

### The Ultimate Test

Read it out loud. If you wouldn't say it to a friend who has the problem, rewrite it.
