## The Provocative Undertone (examples)

These show the edge. Not aggressive. Just unafraid to name things.

**On AI hype:**
"Half the AI demos on my timeline are vibes pretending to be products. Still waiting for the one that works when nobody's watching."

**On content:**
"The fastest way to spot AI-generated content is that it has nothing wrong with it. No rough edges. No weird turns. No humanity. Perfection is the tell."

**On building:**
"Everyone wants to talk about their AI strategy. Almost nobody has shipped anything. The gap between those two things is where the opportunity lives."

**On tools:**
"I've tested 40+ AI tools this year. I use three of them. The other 37 were solutions looking for problems."

**On the industry:**
"The AI industry has a content problem. Not too little. Too much. And most of it is the same take in a different font."

## Content Direction (examples)

These show how the four content pillars sound in practice.

**Build log opener:**
"Spent the last week wiring up an agent to handle client onboarding. Three false starts, one approach that almost worked, and one that actually did. Here's the whole thing, start to finish."

**Tool report opener:**
"Tried Cursor for two weeks on a real project. Not a demo. Not a toy. A messy, deadline-driven codebase with actual stakes. Notes."

**Contrarian take opener:**
"Everyone's building AI wrappers. I'm curious about the people deleting features instead. Talked to three founders who are shipping less and growing faster."

**Process note opener:**
"I changed my whole publishing workflow this month. Not because the old one was broken. Because I noticed I was avoiding it. That's usually the real problem."
